Outreach

Spreading STEM education in our community

MVRT

STEM4KIDS

STEM4Kids was founded with a mission of expanding the world of STEM to those in our community who are underprivileged. Through weekly lessons over the course of a year, we taught students concepts in biology, physics and chemistry.

Sisters in STEM

Founded by our female veterans in 2016 to combat the lack of girls in STEM and encourage more females to join the team. We've had an 80% increase in female attendance and member retention.

Equity Workshops

MVRT annually hosts symposiums in which we invite women in STEM to share how they overcame obstacles within their STEM journeys. We partner with Polygence to offer MVHS students 10% financial assistance.

CADology

CADology was founded to instill a passion for robotics in elementary through middle school aged students using personalized and interactive classes in computer aided design (CAD).

Taiwan

MVRT invited over 25 international students during the summer of 2016 to tour our facilities. We are supporting them as they start their own FRC team.

France Collaboration

In 2023, MVRT visited a summer daycare in rural Saint Gérand, France, to introduce STEM concepts to kids aged 5–9 through a spaghetti and marshmallow tower challenge.

Ohana

A partnership with the Ohana Club at Monta Vista to teach special education students STEM concepts in an interactive, hands-on way. Over 60 students participate.

HeadStart

A week-long summer camp launched in 2016 to build a strong engineering foundation for rookie members. Students learn fundamentals of FRC engineering to assemble a kitbot.

FIRST LEGO League (FLL)

MVRT members have mentored 60 FIRST LEGO League teams since 2003, teaching middle school students engineering and robotics skills.

Hongyun Art

A unique partnership teaching kids STEM through art. Students use Java, Scratch, Python and Arduinos for interactive art projects.

Google Arm & Intel RealSense

Our members worked with Google to build a robot arm to play tic tac toe, and beta-tested Intel's real-sense camera in 2018.

Mali

Through our partnership with iNerde, MVRT helped create a FIRST Global Challenge robotics team in Bamako, Mali. Team Mali won the silver medal for Engineering Design.
